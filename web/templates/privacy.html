Gully XI is a private fantasy cricket site run by the league admin for friends who play in its leagues.

What we keep

  • Your name, email address, and phone number if you give it.
  • Which team you own in each league.
  • Your email choices, with the date you made each one.
  • When you last visited.

Why

  • To run your account: log you in and link you to your team. This is needed to use the site.
  • {{ purposes.service_emails }}: only if you tick that box.
  • {{ purposes.news }}: only if you tick that box, e.g. when a new league or tournament starts.

We don't sell or share your details with anyone. The site is hosted on Railway (servers outside India); emails, once we send them, go through an email delivery service.

Your choices

  • Change your email choices at any time in My account.
  • Download everything we hold about you from My account.
  • Delete your account from My account. Your details are removed; your team's points stay in the league history.

Questions or complaints: ask the league admin.
